## Universal Synthesis Across All Global Traditions

Despite differences in terminology, geography, and diagnostic language, all six global traditional medicine frameworks align on five core pillars:

1. **Moisten and Nourish the Dryness:** Shift diet toward warm, healthy fats (ghee, olive oil, nut oils) and mucilaginous, hydrating herbs to counteract systemic tissue drying.

3. **Protect the Nervous System & Spirit:** Daily grounding practices (self-massage, restorative movement, meditation, or spiritual rituals) act as essential non-pharmacological support for hormonal and emotional balance.

4. **Ignite and Protect Metabolic Fire:** Keep metabolic processes active through warm, cooked meals, bitter roots, and digestive spices to prevent abdominal weight gain, fluid retention, and sluggishness.

5. **Reframe the Stage of Life:** Rather than viewing menopause as a disease of deficiency, these traditional systems universally view it as a transformation—a natural transition into a stage of wisdom, spiritual grounding, and vital energy retention.
